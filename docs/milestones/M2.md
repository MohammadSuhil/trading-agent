- Corporate-action adjustment moves every factor, base rate and the +0.04 vs +0.05 ATR result.
- 70 factors raises multiple-testing risk; deflated metrics arrive in M3.
- `apps/api/main.py` is 619 lines; the ledger refactor touches its contract tests.
