## Decisions the owner must make
- Approve the specific feed list under the archive-only ToS posture.
- Hand-label a gold set (~200 headline→symbol, ~150 event types).
- Keep the poller running for months — RSS has no history; missed polls are lost forever.

## Known traps
- No historical backfill exists, so the IC verdict needs 3–6 months of live data.
- Statistical power is low; "inconclusive, stays DISPLAY_ONLY" is the likely honest outcome.
- Forward returns for IC are wrong until corporate-action adjustment (M2) lands.

## Done when
The crawl-timestamp gate passes on real data and the IC verdict (promote or
display-only) is recorded with its CI.
