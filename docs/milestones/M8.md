**Gate (PLAN.md):** a chart that answers *"is this learning or fooling itself?"* at a
glance. **`random` must be clearly worst after costs — if it isn't, the cost model
is wrong.** Then the committee promotion gate.

## Decisions the owner must make
- Review and **freeze** gate thresholds before data arrives, never after.
- Confirm a free published NIFTY 50 **TRI** source (price index flatters by ~1.2%/yr).

## Known traps
- The promotion gate is wall-clock bound: ~1 resolved trade/session → ~200 sessions ≈ 9.5 months.
- Backfill the three non-LLM arms through the M3 backtester so the chart is populated on day one.
- PIT leakage in in-memory refits (isotonic, posteriors) — the SQL PIT guard doesn't see these.
- Weights barely move in year one by design; that looks like "nothing happening".

## Done when
The chart renders all four arms with CIs, random is worst after costs, and the
promotion gate is frozen and evaluating.
