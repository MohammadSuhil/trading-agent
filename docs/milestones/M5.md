## Decisions the owner must make
- Hours of real inference for prompt iteration (one 25-candidate pass ≈ 1 h of decode).
- Human judgment on the hand-pasted debate: useful reasoning? every number traceable?

## Known traps
- <5% rejection with a local 4-bit model is the biggest overrun risk; may need simpler schemas.
- Dossier rounding vs V2's 0.5% tolerance → false rejections or fabricated numbers slipping through.
- Degraded tiers may need per-tier prompt variants.

## Done when
The gate debate passes with full traceability and the committee writes shadow
events nightly without touching the book.
